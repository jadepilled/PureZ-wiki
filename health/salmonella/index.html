    Salmonella

    SalmonellaMdfrPain lightVomit

    Salmonella is food poisoning from bad food: raw or rotten meat, fruit that has gone off, or eating with bloody hands. Cooking meat properly and keeping your hands clean avoids it.

    It makes you vomit, which empties your stomach and costs water and energy every time. Charcoal tablets clear it and tetracycline also works. If you do nothing it burns out on its own in most cases, as long as you keep eating and drinking to cover the losses.
