disease: mdf_heavymetal1
---
Heavy metal poisoning comes from the cans and containers you eat and drink from. Food heated in a badly damaged cooking pot or pan, or water drunk from a ruined metal container, carries it.

It builds up slowly and then hits hard: pain, shaking hands, deafness, vomiting and a loss of health and water. Chelating tablets are the cure. Keep your cooking gear and canteens in good repair to avoid it.
