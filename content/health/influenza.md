The flu starts as a cold and grows if you stay cold and wet, or you catch it from a sick player coughing nearby. Getting warm and dry early is the best prevention.

You cough and sneeze, which gives away your position, and you lose stamina faster. Left alone in the cold it becomes pneumonia. Tetracycline cures it; multivitamins and staying warm help your immune system do the rest. Covering your face lowers the chance of passing it on.
