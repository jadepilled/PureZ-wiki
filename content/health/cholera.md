Cholera comes from dirty water. Drinking straight from ponds, rivers or puddles, or from a container that was filled there, can carry it. Eating or drinking with bloody hands does the same. Wells and pumps are safe, and so is water that has been boiled or treated with purification tablets.

Once it takes hold you vomit at random, losing water and energy each time, and you get thirsty much faster than normal. It will not kill you on its own, but the dehydration will if you let it run. A dose of tetracycline clears it; keep drinking clean water while it works. A strong immune system can also fight it off over time.
