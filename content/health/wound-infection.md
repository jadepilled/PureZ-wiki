A wound infection starts when a cut is closed with a dirty rag or bandage. Disinfect rags and bandages with disinfectant spray or alcohol before using them, or use a sterile bandage, and it will not happen.

The first stage is mild: shivering hands and occasional pain. It is easy to miss. Tetracycline cures it at this stage, and that is the time to take it, because untreated it becomes a severe infection that drains health.
