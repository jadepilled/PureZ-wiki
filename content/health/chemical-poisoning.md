Chemical poisoning comes from the toxic gas in contaminated zones and from gas grenades. A gas mask with a working filter, or a full NBC suit, keeps it out. Food and water left in a gas zone can also be contaminated.

Mild poisoning gives a cough and blurred vision and wears off once you leave the gas. It only becomes serious if you stay in or keep going back. The antidote injector clears all stages.
