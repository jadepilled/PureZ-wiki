A cold is the first stage of the flu. You catch it by being cold or soaked for too long, or from someone who already has it. It is the game's warning that you need to warm up.

Symptoms are sneezing and a slow drain on energy. Get dry, light a fire or put on better clothing, and it usually passes by itself. Ignore it in the cold and it turns into influenza. Tetracycline cures it outright.
